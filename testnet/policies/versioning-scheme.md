# Testnet Versioning Scheme & Access Model

## Deployment Versioning

Bridgelet Core testnet deployments follow semantic versioning tags aligned with major protocol upgrades:

- `testnet-v1.x`: Initial MVP deployment for ephemeral account sweep validation.
- `testnet-v2.x`: Multi-asset support and governance dispute resolution modules.

## Permissionless Access Model

The Stellar Testnet deployment is entirely public and permissionless. Integrators are encouraged to test contract interactions freely while adhering to RPC rate-limiting guidelines.
